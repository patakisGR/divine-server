The Legend

The Fall of Anax

A king named Anax once bribed Pythia — the Oracle of Delphi — for knowledge no mortal was meant to hold. The gods did not forgive lightly.

Banished to a dimension beyond sight, Anax did not die. He festered. His corruption seeped through the barrier between worlds, forming a rift that bleeds darkness into our realm — twisting the land, corrupting its creatures, and warping reality itself.

You are one of the few willing to step into the rift. Fight through his champions. Break his hold on this world. Face Anax.
